Our Story

BORN IN WERRIBEE.
SMASHING IT IN THE WEST.

Phatburgerz started in Werribee because we wanted a proper smash burger in the west — and couldn't find one. We built it ourselves, took it to the streets, and the community showed up hard. Those early days cooking out of Synnot Street shaped everything about who we are.

Now we've levelled up to our new home on Ballarat Rd, Caroline Springs — same energy, same love, same smashed-to-perfection patties. You're not just a customer. You're fam.
